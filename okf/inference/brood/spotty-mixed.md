Irregular brood with empty cells scattered among capped and uncapped — no clear drone dominance.

# Candidates

- [Queenright](/states/queenright.md) (confidence 0.25) — failing queen
- [Queenless](/states/queenless.md) (confidence 0.2) — queen died or failed

# Notes

Differential diagnosis — do NOT jump to a conclusion. Check: (1) Varroa: alcohol wash or sugar shake. High mite load + spotty brood = virus. (2) Queen: look for eggs. No eggs + spotty = queen failing or gone. (3) Disease: smell (AFB = rotten), rope test, check for chalkbrood mummies. (4) Pesticide: ask about recent spraying in area. (5) Cold snap: if recent dip below 10°C during brood rearing, cold-damaged brood.
