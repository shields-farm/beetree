# Brood

* [Absent](absent.md) - No brood of any kind on the frame.
* [Center Cluster Only](center-cluster-only.md) - Brood tightly clustered in the center of the frame, empty cells at the periphery.
* [Drone Only Pupal](drone-only-pupal.md) - Only drone pupal brood visible — no worker larvae or eggs.
* [Peripheral Brood](peripheral-brood.md) - Brood on the frame edges but not center — unusual, center cells empty.
* [Solid Capped](solid-capped.md) - Frame fully capped, tight pattern edge to edge.
* [Solid Scattered Uncapped](solid-scattered-uncapped.md) - Mostly solid pattern with scattered uncapped or empty cells throughout.
* [Spotty Drone Dominant](spotty-drone-dominant.md) - Spotty brood with mostly drone cells — bullet-shaped cappings raised above the frame surface.
* [Spotty Mixed](spotty-mixed.md) - Irregular brood with empty cells scattered among capped and uncapped — no clear drone dominance.
