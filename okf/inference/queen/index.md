# Queen

* [Eggs Present Multiple Per Cell](eggs-present-multiple-per-cell.md) - Multiple eggs per cell, eggs laid on cell walls, not at the base.
* [Eggs Present Single](eggs-present-single.md) - Single egg per cell, upright at the base — mated queen recently active.
* [Emergency Cells](emergency-cells.md) - Queen cells raised from worker cells, scattered, rougher/shorter than swarm or supersedure cells.
* [No Queen No Eggs No Cells](no-queen-no-eggs-no-cells.md) - No queen spotted, no eggs, no queen cells of any kind.
* [Queen Capped](queen-capped.md) - A capped queen cell is present — the new queen is developing inside and will emerge soon.
* [Queen Cups Empty](queen-cups-empty.md) - Empty queen cups on the bottom bars of frames — no egg, no larva, no royal jelly. Just the cup.
* [Queen Not Found Eggs Present](queen-not-found-eggs-present.md) - Couldn't spot the queen, but eggs are present and normal (single per cell).
* [Queen Not Found No Eggs](queen-not-found-no-eggs.md) - Couldn't find the queen and no eggs visible.
* [Queen Spotted Marked](queen-spotted-marked.md) - Queen located, marked with a color dot on her thorax.
* [Queen Spotted Unmarked](queen-spotted-unmarked.md) - Queen located but unmarked.
* [Supersedure Cells](supersedure-cells.md) - 1-3 queen cells on the face of the frame (not bottom edge).
* [Swarm Cells](swarm-cells.md) - Queen cells along the bottom edge of frames — multiple, peanut-shaped.
* [Virgin Queen](virgin-queen.md) - Small, fast-moving queen with no eggs laid yet — may not be mated.
