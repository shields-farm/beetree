# Description

Empty queen cups on the bottom bars of frames — no egg, no larva, no royal jelly. Just the cup.

# Candidates

- [Queenright](/states/queenright.md) (confidence 0.85)

# Notes

Queen cups are NOT queen cells. They are play cups — workers draw them on the bottom bars as a normal spring behavior, essentially practicing for swarm season. EMPTY queen cups are harmless. They do not mean the colony is going to swarm. The key distinction: a queen cup becomes a queen cell only when an egg is laid in it and royal jelly is added. Check again at your next inspection — if the cups are still empty, nothing has changed. If there's an egg or larva inside, NOW you have a queen cell and need to assess swarm vs supersedure. Do NOT destroy empty queen cups — the workers will just rebuild them and you'll lose the signal of when they actually start using them.
