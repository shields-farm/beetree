# Description

Queen cells raised from worker cells, scattered, rougher/shorter than swarm or supersedure cells.

# Candidates

- [Queenless](/states/queenless.md) (confidence 0.8) — colony lost its queen and is raising an emergency replacement

# Notes

Emergency cells. The queen died or was lost, and the colony is raising a new one from worker larvae. If you have a mated queen available, you can requeen directly (introduce her in a cage). If not, let them raise their own — but check they have eggs/young larvae to work with. Emergency queens from older larvae are lower quality (shorter abdomen, fewer ovarioles).
