# Description

Hive empty, no dead bees, some honey/pollen remaining, brood left behind.

# Diagnosis

Absconding. The entire colony left — usually due to stress (mites, predators, disturbance, starvation).

# Notes

Absconding is common with packages or small colonies under severe stress (varroa, small hive beetle pressure, pesticide, or even repeated disturbance). The brood left behind tells you they left in a hurry. Clean the hive, check comb, and restart with a new package or nuc. Address whatever drove them out first.
