# Description

Chewed comb, nesting material (shredded wood/leaves), mouse droppings, foul smell.

# Diagnosis

Mouse infestation during winter. Mouse entered through the entrance and nested in the hive.

# Notes

Mice enter in winter when the cluster is tight and can't defend the entrance. Install mouse guards (entrance reducer or metal grid) in fall. Clean out nesting material and droppings, assess comb damage, replace destroyed frames. The colony usually survives but may be weakened by the disturbance.
