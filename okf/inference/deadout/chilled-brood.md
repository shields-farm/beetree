# Description

Grey or brown pupae/larvae on the bottom board, brood left uncovered and cold.

# Diagnosis

Chilled brood. Colony was too small to cover the brood nest during a cold spell.

# Notes

The cluster contracted during cold weather and left brood on the edges to chill. Reduce the hive volume (combine, remove empty boxes, add a follower board) so the cluster can cover the remaining brood. If the colony is very small, combine with a stronger one.
