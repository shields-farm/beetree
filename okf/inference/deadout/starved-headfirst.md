# Description

Dead bees head-first in empty cells, no honey stores remaining.

# Diagnosis

Starvation. Bees crawled into cells looking for food and died there.

# Notes

The colony ran out of honey. This is preventable — either they didn't have enough stores going into winter, or they consumed them faster than expected (small cluster, cold spell). Check heft going into winter; feed syrup (2:1) in fall until they won't take more. Emergency fondant in winter if heft is light.
