# Description

Large pile of dead bees at the entrance, tongues sticking out, many dead outside the hive.

# Diagnosis

Pesticide poisoning. Acute exposure — bees died returning from foraging.

# Notes

Tongues extended is classic pesticide kill. Check with neighbors about recent spraying. Report to your state agricultural department if warranted. Move hives if exposure is chronic. The colony may recover if the queen survived and enough bees remain — check in a few days for egg-laying and population recovery.
