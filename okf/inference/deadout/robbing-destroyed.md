# Description

Torn cappings, honey cells ripped open, dead bees, disorganized comb, no stores remaining.

# Diagnosis

Robbing. Other bees entered and stripped the colony's honey, fighting killed the colony.

# Notes

Robbing happens during dearth or when a colony is weak and can't defend itself. Prevent with entrance reducers during dearth. Never spill honey or syrup near hives. Open feeding attracts robbers. If you see robbing starting, reduce entrances immediately and cover the hive with a wet sheet to confuse the robbers.
