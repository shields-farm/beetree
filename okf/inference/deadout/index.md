# Deadout

* [Absconded](absconded.md) - Hive empty, no dead bees, some honey/pollen remaining, brood left behind.
* [Chilled Brood](chilled-brood.md) - Grey or brown pupae/larvae on the bottom board, brood left uncovered and cold.
* [Dead Queen Only](dead-queen-only.md) - Only the queen is dead, rest of the colony appears normal.
* [Mice Damage](mice-damage.md) - Chewed comb, nesting material (shredded wood/leaves), mouse droppings, foul smell.
* [Nosema Dysentery](nosema-dysentery.md) - Brown streaks (dysentery/feces) on the front of the hive, frames, and top bars.
* [Pesticide Tongues Out](pesticide-tongues-out.md) - Large pile of dead bees at the entrance, tongues sticking out, many dead outside the hive.
* [Robbing Destroyed](robbing-destroyed.md) - Torn cappings, honey cells ripped open, dead bees, disorganized comb, no stores remaining.
* [Starved Headfirst](starved-headfirst.md) - Dead bees head-first in empty cells, no honey stores remaining.
