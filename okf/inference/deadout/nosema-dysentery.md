# Description

Brown streaks (dysentery/feces) on the front of the hive, frames, and top bars.

# Diagnosis

Nosema infection. Dysentery from gut pathogen (Nosema ceranae).

# Notes

Nosema causes dysentery — brown streaks are bee feces. More common in winter/early spring when bees can't fly to defecate. Treat with Fumagillin (Fumidil-B) in syrup, or let the colony clear it naturally once they can take cleansing flights. Check if colony is queenright once they're flying.
