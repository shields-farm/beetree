# Description

Dark brown comb — many brood cycles, cocoons built up, cells slightly smaller.

# Action

Rotate out this season. Move to the outside of the brood box or replace with new foundation/frame.

# Notes

Old dark comb: cells are smaller (bees raised in them are slightly smaller), cocoon layers have built up, and pesticide/acaricide residues (including any treatments you applied) are concentrated in the wax. Rotate by moving dark frames to the outside position so the queen stops laying in them, then remove when empty of brood. Replace with fresh foundation or let bees draw new comb on starter strips.
