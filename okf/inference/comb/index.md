# Comb

* [Black Old](black-old.md) - Black comb — very old, cells significantly narrowed, heavy cocoon and propolis buildup.
* [Brace Comb](brace-comb.md) - Small wax connections between frame top bars or between frames and the box wall — worker-built structural supp
* [Burr Comb](burr-comb.md) - Wax built in unwanted places — between frames, on top bars, on the inner cover, around feeders.
* [Cross Comb](cross-comb.md) - Comb built across multiple frames or at right angles to the frame direction — frames stuck together.
* [Dark Brown](dark-brown.md) - Dark brown comb — many brood cycles, cocoons built up, cells slightly smaller.
* [Distorted Cells](distorted-cells.md) - Cells are irregular — uneven walls, mixed sizes, wavy pattern.
* [Drone Comb In Worker Area](drone-comb-in-worker-area.md) - Drone comb (larger cells) appearing in the worker brood area.
* [Golden Light](golden-light.md) - Light golden color — several brood cycles.
* [Golden Medium](golden-medium.md) - Medium golden-brown — mid-life comb.
* [Light Cream](light-cream.md) - Slightly off-white — one or two brood cycles.
* [Wax Moth Damage](wax-moth-damage.md) - Silken webbing, tunnels through comb, larvae visible. Comb is being destroyed.
* [White New](white-new.md) - Freshly drawn white wax — never had brood raised in it.
