# Description

Drone comb (larger cells) appearing in the worker brood area.

# Action

Monitor. Some drone comb is natural (~10-15% of comb). If excessive, the queen may be failing or the colony is crowded.

# Notes

Drone comb is not a problem in itself — drones are normal. But if you see a lot of drone comb being drawn in worker areas, it can signal the queen is out of space or the colony is preparing to swarm. Also: varroa prefer drone cells, so large patches of drone comb are a varroa hotspot.
