# Description

Black comb — very old, cells significantly narrowed, heavy cocoon and propolis buildup.

# Action

Replace immediately. This comb is overdue and potentially harmful to colony health.

# Notes

Black comb should not be in the brood nest. Bees raised in old narrow cells are smaller and potentially less healthy. Residue accumulation is at peak. Remove the frame, render the wax (or discard if treated with acaricides), and replace with new foundation or a clean drawn frame.
