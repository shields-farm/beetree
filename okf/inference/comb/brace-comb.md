# Description

Small wax connections between frame top bars or between frames and the box wall — worker-built structural supports.

# Action

Break brace comb gently during inspection to separate frames. Minor brace comb is normal and unavoidable.

# Notes

Brace comb (small connections between top bars) is different from burr comb (free-standing comb in unwanted spaces). Minor brace comb is normal — bees connect frames for structural stability. Excessive brace comb that makes frames hard to separate may indicate the hive is vibrating or shifting, or the bees are being particularly industrious. Use a hive tool to break connections before pulling frames.
