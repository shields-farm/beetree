# Description

Comb built across multiple frames or at right angles to the frame direction — frames stuck together.

# Action

Cut the cross comb out carefully during inspection. Realign frames to proper spacing. If the colony persists, use foundation or starter strips to guide comb direction. Cross comb makes inspections impossible and should be corrected early.

# Notes

Cross comb usually happens when bees are drawing comb without a guide (no foundation, no starter strip, or frames spaced too wide). Fix early — the longer it goes, the more brood and honey are in the wrong places, and the harder it is to fix without losing bees/brood. Some genetics are more prone to it than others.
