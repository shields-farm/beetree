# Varroa

* [Deformed Wings](deformed-wings.md) - Bees with shriveled, crumpled, or stunted wings emerging from cells.
* [Hairless Black Bees](hairless-black-bees.md) - Bees that appear hairless, dark/black, shiny — sometimes trembling on frames.
* [K Wing](k-wing.md) - Wings held at an unnatural angle (not folded flat over the abdomen), resembling a K shape.
* [No Visible Symptoms](no-visible-symptoms.md) - Bees look healthy — no wing deformities, no hairless bees, no visible mites.
* [Parasitic Mite Syndrome](parasitic-mite-syndrome.md) - Multiple symptoms: deformed wings + hairless bees + spotty brood + reduced population.
* [Phoretic Mites Visible](phoretic-mites-visible.md) - Varroa mites visible on the backs of adult bees — small brown/red discs on the thorax or abdomen.
