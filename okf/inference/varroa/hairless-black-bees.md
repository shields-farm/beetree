# Description

Bees that appear hairless, dark/black, shiny — sometimes trembling on frames.

# Candidates

- [Dwindling](/states/dwindling.md) (confidence 0.75) — Chronic Bee Paralysis Virus (CBPV)

# Notes

Hairless black syndrome = CBPV. Often seen in large colonies under varroa pressure. The bees lose body hair, appear shiny and black, and may tremble or be expelled by guard bees. Treat for varroa (reduces the viral load). The colony may recover if mite pressure is brought down quickly.
