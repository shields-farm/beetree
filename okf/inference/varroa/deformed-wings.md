Bees with shriveled, crumpled, or stunted wings emerging from cells.

- [Dwindling](/states/dwindling.md) (confidence 0.85) — Deformed Wing Virus (DWV) — vectored by varroa

# Notes

DWV symptoms mean high varroa load has been present for weeks. The virus causes wing deformity and reduced lifespan. ACTION: treat for varroa immediately. If it's during the honey flow, use a treatment that doesn't contaminate honey (formic acid = Mite Away Quick Strips, or thymol after flow). Confirm with an alcohol wash or sugar shake. If >2% mite load: treat. If >5%: colony is in crisis.
