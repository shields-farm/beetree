## KEY EXAM TIPS (From the UGA Website)

### Journeyman Practical Exam
- Questions 1–6 must score **90%+** (disorder ID, insect ID, beekeeping situation)
- Questions 7–10 must score **70%** (colony status interpretation, anatomy drawing, flower parts, pollinator conservation)
- **Question 10** is drawn directly from *Nature's Best Hope* (Tallamy)
- Disorder ID: Varroa, SHB, AFB, EFB, laying worker, drone layer, SHB larvae vs wax moth larvae
- Insect ID: 14+ species including bee mimics — know that flies have 1 pair of wings, bees/wasps have 2

### Master Written Exam (New for 2026)
- Includes **essay questions** requiring practical beekeeping experience
- Includes **peer-reviewed article comprehension** — 5 articles posted before exam, questions from 1 of the 5
- Topic areas: toxicology (LD50, insecticide classes, label reading), pollination, honey hoarding biology (Farrar), honey bee relatives, honey bee biology (eusociality, polyandry, chromosomes, races, morphology, nest temp), honey preparation (HMF, labeling, comb honey), pollinator conservation, honey bee health (IPM, all diseases/pests)

### Master Craftsman Oral Exam
- Tested by 3+ examiners including Extension apiculturist
- One specific area of candidate's choice + general bee biology/beekeeping
- Zero fail tolerance on subspecialties 1 (disease diagnosis/treatment) and 2 (pest diagnosis/treatment)
