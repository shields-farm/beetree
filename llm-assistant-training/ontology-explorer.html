Ontology Explorer

Click a node for its description and connections. Scroll to zoom in, drag to pan around. The layout is hive-inspired: observation nodes cluster like comb cells, states are at the heart, threats are on the outer ring.

How It Works

Center
Hive → Colony → states. The core entity.
Inner ring
Colony states (yellow diamonds) — Queenright, DroneLayer, SwarmPrep, etc.
Observation ring
What you see at the frame face (teal). Each has a rule set (orange hexagon) that maps patterns to states.
Pattern cells
Individual observation patterns (cyan). Click one to see its diagnosis and confidence values.
Outer rings
Threat species (red) and season phases (purple). The environment and calendar.
